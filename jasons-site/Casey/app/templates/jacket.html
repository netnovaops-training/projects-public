THE RAIL IS THE PAGE. The strongest visual object in her world is not a chart — it is a ribbon rack: her career already gets worn as coloured bars in a row. So milestones are bars mounted on a rail, not dots on a line. Bar height carries tier, horizontal position carries date, and the years run underneath as a scale. Structure is information. RED AND BLUE ARE KIND, NOT SEVERITY. Red changes what she IS — commissioned, promoted, pinned. Blue builds toward the next red one — a certification, an instructor card, a quarterly win. Saying "major" and "minor" out loud would imply the blue ones matter less, and they are precisely what make the red ones possible, so the legend says so. #}
