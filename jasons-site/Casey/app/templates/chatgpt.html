Bring in what you have already told it

There is no way to log into your ChatGPT account from here and pull your chats — an API key gives access to the models, not to your conversations. Nothing can be wired up to do it. What works is the export you run yourself:

In ChatGPT: Settings → Data controls → Export data. It emails you a link to a .zip. Upload that zip here, whole — you do not need to unpack it.

The export is held in memory only while you pick a conversation, and is never written to disk. It is your whole chat history, most of which has nothing to do with your career, and there is no reason for this machine to keep a copy of all of it.

Let ChatGPT read and write this record

{% if have_token %}

Set up once, and the ChatGPT you already pay for can see your awards, your progress and your record — and write to them. Anything it changes shows up here and on your phone, because there is only one record.

  1. In ChatGPT: Explore GPTs → Create → Configure → Create new action.
  2. Under Schema, choose Import from URL and paste:
    {{ public_url or 'set ZEE_PUBLIC_URL first' }}/actions/openapi.json
  3. Under Authentication: API Key, Auth Type Bearer, and paste the token from your .env (ZEE_ACTION_TOKEN).
  4. Save the GPT. Ask it "what awards am I chasing?" to check it.

That token is the only thing standing between the open internet and this record. Treat it like a password: do not paste it into a chat, and if it ever gets out, change it in .env and restart.
